About {{ site_name }}

{{ site_name }} is an independent website that makes it easier to prepare India Post account opening forms. Many people, especially first-time depositors, senior citizens and those helping relatives, make small mistakes on handwritten forms that lead to rejections and repeat visits. We built a simple tool that places your answers neatly onto the form so you can print, check and sign it.

What we offer

  • A free form-filling tool that produces a print-ready PDF.
  • Plain-language guides on post office accounts, savings schemes and nominee details.

What we are not

We are not India Post, a bank, or a government body, and we do not open accounts or accept deposits. Always confirm current rules, forms and rates with your nearest post office or the official India Post website.

Our approach

We write our guides in simple language, review them when schemes change, and do not ask for more personal information than the tool needs to function. Questions or corrections are welcome on the contact page.
