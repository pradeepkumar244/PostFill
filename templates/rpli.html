How the calculator works

The rate tables give the premium for every ₹1,000 of sum assured, separately for monthly, quarterly and yearly payment. Your instalment is that rate multiplied by your sum assured divided by 1,000. For example, a monthly rate of 5.10 on ₹1,00,000 gives 5.10 × 100 = ₹510 a month.

The bonus is estimated as sum assured ÷ 1,000 × bonus rate × policy term in years, and the maturity amount is the sum assured plus that bonus. The bonus rate is declared by the Department of Posts every year and is not guaranteed, so you can edit it above or clear it to hide the bonus lines. Terminal bonus, if any, is not included.

About RPLI

Rural Postal Life Insurance is a government-backed life insurance scheme for people living in rural areas. The endowment plan pays the sum assured plus bonus when you reach the chosen maturity age, or to your nominee if you die earlier. You can choose to stop paying premiums at age 35, 40, 45, 50, 55, 58 or 60, and entry age is {{ min_age }} to {{ max_age }}. Terms shorter than 5 years are not offered, which is why some maturity ages are missing for older applicants.

Tips before you apply

  • Compare the three payment modes. Paying yearly costs less in total than paying monthly, but needs a larger amount at once.
  • Compare two or three maturity ages. A longer term has a lower instalment, but you pay for more years.
  • Add a nominee and keep your age proof ready, since premium depends on your age at entry.
